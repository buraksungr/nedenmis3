Nedenmiş Studio — third-party components

Remotion is used under its license for individuals / small teams. Check the current terms before commercial redistribution or use by larger organizations: https://www.remotion.dev/docs/license . The upstream package licenses are retained with the installed modules.

FFmpeg and FFprobe are separate, unmodified executables from @ffmpeg-installer and @ffprobe-installer. Build information and enabled codecs are available via -version and -buildconf. See the license and upstream binary/source origin in each installed platform package and https://ffmpeg.org/legal.html . Remotion also ships its platform compositor binaries and their license notice.

Electron includes Chromium and Node.js; see LICENSE.electron.txt and LICENSES.chromium.html in the install directory. React and Lucide are distributed under their respective open-source licenses. Inter and Libre Caslon Text are bundled locally under the SIL Open Font License.

Illustrated covers and the studio banner are AI-generated artwork for the application library. Seed projects are editable editorial examples, not Google-generated videos. The procedural score and ambient/Foley signals are synthesized locally; no third-party recordings or music are downloaded. Imported media must be owned or appropriately licensed by its user.

Whisper, stable-ts and CPU PyTorch are optionally installed into a separate local Python environment. Model weights are not in this installer. Text generation uses the server-side Google Gen AI JavaScript SDK (@google/genai, Apache-2.0; https://github.com/googleapis/js-genai). Google Gemini is a separate online service subject to Google's terms and pricing.

Storyboard image generation uses the separate Pollinations online service. An approved scene prompt (or its visual description) and a fixed style suffix are sent to the selected endpoint. A saved Pollinations secret key is used only for the current API; account charges and service terms may apply. Local uploads do not require Pollinations. API documentation: https://github.com/pollinations/pollinations/blob/main/APIDOCS.md

Full-stage vector scenes use the selected OpenRouter or Google Gemini text service and its terms. No model weights or QA illustrations are bundled. SVG path validation uses svg-pathdata; the unmodified resvg-wasm module (MPL-2.0, source: https://github.com/yisibl/resvg-js/tree/v2.6.2) performs a local paint validation. Historical science-atlas layouts remain available for playback only. Preserve media/atlas/credits in old project backups for attribution of any previously acquired art. New scenes do not acquire Iconify icons.
